Source: https://hla.alleles.org/pages/p%26g_groups/g_groups/

G groups concern antigen-binding-exon nucleotide identity. Their construction can infer missing sequence for incompletely sequenced alleles, so group overlap is compatibility evidence, not direct sequence verification. Definitions consulted 2026-09-16; actual membership is pinned to IPD 3.65.0 in hla-analysis/source/imgt/wmda/.

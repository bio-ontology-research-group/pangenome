# Analysis goal — completion record

All five requested analysis deliverables have been produced and reviewed against the original objective below. The 19-stage local workflow completed successfully from frozen source data and archived remote measurements. See [the final report](FINAL_REPORT.md), [completion audit](COMPLETION_AUDIT.md), and `results/completion_evidence.json`.

Completion does not claim a confirmed novel allele, functional/regulatory effect, resolved pathogenic RCCX configuration or UKB association. Candidate uncertainty, absent reads, possible artefacts and future validations are explicit in the deliverables. UKB execution remains post-hackathon, as agreed.

## Original objective

1. A frozen, quality-controlled panel. Reconcile donor identities and known relatives, flag fragmentation and clipping, record software/database versions, and define which haplotypes are
     eligible for each comparison.

  2. A leakage-controlled typing benchmark. Compare gene sequence, gene-level bundles, regional bundles and gene-content features. Fit any learned representation on training donors only—or
     explicitly describe the experiment as using a fixed reference panel. Report resolution, call rate, ambiguity, rare-allele performance and cohort transfer. Use experimental labels where
     available; investigate disagreements with the assembly baseline.

  3. A catalogue of information beyond conventional labels. Find sequence, copy-number and arrangement differences within otherwise matching HLA types. Separate differences beyond the three-
     gene DR–DQ combination from differences beyond all available classical HLA calls, including DRB3/4/5. Include regulatory/flanking variation and, as a distinct MHC analysis, C4/RCCX.

  4. An evidence-ranked candidate assessment. Recheck candidates against a current, frozen allele database and published literature. Classify each as known variation, a plausible novel
     allele/structure, an annotation or assembly artefact, or unresolved. Validate leading candidates with matched reads, alternative assemblies or inheritance evidence where available.

  5. A final report and reproducible workflow. State what bundles add over simpler sequence-based methods, what remains unidentified, and what can realistically be genotyped in UKB. Include
     stable candidate definitions and the proposed association tests.
